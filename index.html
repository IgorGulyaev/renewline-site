Renewline
Renewal pipeline for IT resellers

The renewal you lose is the one nobody built a quote for.

Licences, security subscriptions, care packs and support contracts all expire on their own dates, sit in different portals, and end up in one spreadsheet that somebody opens when they remember. Renewline reads that spreadsheet, tells you what is about to lapse, and writes the renewal quote for you to send.

Built for resellers and VARs who sell licences but don't run an RMM — the people every lifecycle tool on the market can't onboard, because there is no agent anywhere to read data from.

How it works

Four steps, and the fourth one is the point.

  1. 01

    Drop in your export

    CSV from a PSA, a distributor portal, or the spreadsheet you actually use. Columns are matched automatically — Client Name, Expiry Date, Sell Price and the other forty spellings of the same thing. No mapping screen, no integration, no call.

  2. 02

    Everything lands on one timeline

    Day/month dates read as day/month. 1.234,56 € and £1,234.56 both become money. Anything unreadable is listed and skipped — never quietly guessed at.

  3. 03

    You see what is at risk, in pounds

    Not a count of tickets. The value of everything expiring inside 60 days that nobody has sent a quote for.

  4. 04

    The quote and the email are already written

    Pick a customer, set your uplift, get a priced quote document and a client-ready email. Copy, send, mark it sent. That is the whole job.

Positioning

Why another one of these.

Lifecycle and renewal tools exist, and the good ones are good. They were built for managed service providers: they read hardware and warranty data from an agent installed on every endpoint, they price per endpoint, and they sell on annual terms. If you resell licences rather than manage estates, none of that describes you.

Built for the MSP model

  • Data comes from an RMM or PSA integration
  • Priced per endpoint or per managed device
  • Annual commitment, cancellation via your account manager
  • Hardware warranties first; software licences are a manual field

Renewline

  • Data comes from your spreadsheet. Nothing to install
  • Flat rate by lines and users, not by endpoint
  • Month to month. Cancel in the app, no conversation
  • Licences and subscriptions first; warranties handled the same way

Honest limits

What Renewline is not.

Worth knowing before you spend twenty minutes on it.

  • It is pre-release. No public sign-up yet, and no paying customers to point at.
  • It does not integrate with ConnectWise, Autotask, Halo or any RMM. That is deliberate for now — the import is the integration.
  • It does not invoice, take payment, or place orders with your distributor.
  • It does not discover assets. If a renewal is not in a file you have, Renewline cannot know about it.
  • Renewal pricing comes from your own last-sold prices plus an uplift you set. It does not fetch live distributor pricing.
